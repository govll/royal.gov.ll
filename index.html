The Crown

The role of the Monarchy

The Monarch of Links Land serves as Head of State and a symbol of national unity and continuity. Under the Constitution, the Crown fulfils a vital ceremonial and constitutional role, acting always on the advice of the elected government and in accordance with the will of the people of Links Land.

The monarchy is hereditary. The throne passes according to the rules of primogeniture, with any changes to the line of succession requiring parliamentary approval.

The Constitution

Founded on democratic principles

Links Land is a Democratic-Constitutional Monarchy founded on the principles of democracy, liberty, and equality. The Constitution, which came into effect on 16 May 2025, defines the governance of the nation and protects the rights of all citizens.

The monarchy operates strictly within the framework of that Constitution. All acts of the Monarch are performed on the advice of the President and the government, ensuring full democratic accountability.
